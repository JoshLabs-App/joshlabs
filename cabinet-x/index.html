Philosophy

01

Decide with your eyes, not your imagination.

Kitchen renovations are expensive and hard to reverse. Cabinet X lets you see the result before you commit — real AI visualization, your actual kitchen, your actual room.

厨房改造贵且难以反悔。Cabinet X 让你在决定之前先看到结果，用你真实的厨房照片做 AI 效果图。

02

Compare six, not one.

Generate up to six cabinet finishes in one run and swipe through before/after results. The best decision comes from comparison, not guessing.

一次生成最多六种方案，滑动对比改造前后。最好的决定来自比较，而不是猜测。

03

Professional, from quote to close.

Kitchen designers and contractors can share AI renders directly with clients, add a company watermark, and present options in one tap.

厨房设计师和承包商可以直接把效果图分享给客户，加上公司水印，一键展示方案。

Features · 功能

iOS & Android

Photo in, design out

Snap or import a kitchen photo and get an AI redesign with your chosen cabinet style instantly.

13+ door styles

Flat and shaker finishes from the Josh Kitchen catalog, plus optional custom door photo upload.

Batch compare · 批量对比

Generate up to six finishes in one run and swipe through before/after results.

Before & after slider

Interactive slider to review the original room against the AI visualization.

Save & share · 保存与分享

Export images to Photos, share with clients, and add an optional company watermark.

Optional cloud sync

Google sign-in syncs design history and preferences across devices (optional).

English & 中文
