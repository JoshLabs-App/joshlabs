Philosophy

01

No subscription. Not now, not later.

Most bookkeeping apps charge monthly for features you needed on day one. JoshMoney is free — core books, reports, invoices, payroll, all of it.

大多数记账软件按月收费。JoshMoney 现在免费，核心功能全部包含。

02

Offline-first. Your data, your device.

Open and post entries without an account. Your books live on your device. Cloud sync with Google or Apple is optional — you opt in, not out.

无需注册即可记账，数据在你手机上。Google / Apple 同步是可选项，你选择开启。

03

Built for Canada & the US.

T2125, GST/HST summaries, Schedule C exports — not generic spreadsheets but forms your accountant actually recognizes. Reference only; always confirm before filing.

T2125、GST/HST、Schedule C 导出，不是通用表，是你会计认识的表。仅供参考，报税前需确认。

What's inside · 功能

iOS & Android

Live reports · 实况报表

Profit, tax collected, and receivables update with your entries — not a static month-end snapshot.

Tax-prep assistant · 报税助手

Reminders, short notes beside the numbers, and exports to use before filing.

Business insights · 经营分析

Income vs. expenses breakdown, top categories, and a clear view of how the business is doing.

Invoices · 发票

Create invoices, share PDF, and track partial payments on one screen.

Payroll · 工资

Payslips with tax estimates for reference. Issued slips stay as recorded.

Shared books · 多人记账

Invite a helper to the same books. Owner and helpers on one ledger.

To-dos · 待办

Tasks and reminders that follow remittance, pay, and receivable dates.

CA & US tax exports · 优化报税表

Canada: T2125, GST summary. US: Schedule C reference. Confirm with your accountant.

Offline-first · 离线

Core bookkeeping works without an account or connection. Optional cloud sync when you want backup.
