Quiet rest · 安静歇息 · v1.0.0

Selah.my

A pause in a world that never stops.

Selah is a Hebrew pause mark — a breath between verses. This app is the same: nature scenes, ambient sound, and a rest timer. Open it, set a duration, and stop. No notifications, no feed, nothing to do.

Selah 是希伯来文的停顿符号——诗节之间的一口气。这款 App 也是：自然场景、环境声、休息计时。打开，设个时间，停下来。没有推送，没有信息流，什么都不用做。

Philosophy

01

Rest is not wasted time.

We built Selah.my because most "relaxation" apps want your attention. Selah wants nothing from you. Open, breathe, set a timer, stop.

大多数"放松"应用抢你的注意力。Selah 什么都不要。打开，深呼吸，定时，停下来。

02

No account. No profile. No feed.

Selah.my opens and works. There is nothing to log in to, nothing to follow, no metrics tracked. Optional sign-in exists only to sync your preferences — you opt in, not out.

无需登录，无关注，无数据追踪。可选登录仅用于偏好同步，你选择开启。

03

Nature, sound, and a timer. That's the whole app.

No AI suggestions. No wellness score. No streak counter. A beautiful scene, ambient sound, and a countdown. Simple by intention, not by accident.

没有 AI 建议，没有健康评分，没有连续打卡计数。一幅自然场景、一段环境声、一个倒计时。简单是设计，不是偷懒。

Features · 功能

iOS & Android

Nature scenes · 自然场景

Curated landscape backgrounds that give your eyes somewhere to rest.

Ambient sound · 环境声

Rain, forest, ocean — layered ambient audio that fills the silence without demanding attention.

Rest timer · 休息计时

Set how long you want to pause. The app ends gently, no alarm blast.

Offline-first · 离线

Works without a connection. Scenes and sounds load once, no stream to buffer.

No account required · 无需账号

Opens immediately. Optional sign-in for syncing preferences only.

No notifications · 不推送

Selah.my does not interrupt. It waits until you come to it.

Name · 名字的意思

Selah (סֶלָה) is a Hebrew musical or liturgical term — a pause, a breath. It appears 74 times in the Psalms.
