概述

截图译由 Joshua Zeng（JoshLabs，加拿大）开发。我们的原则是：你的截图只在手机本机处理，不会上传。

照片与截图

在你授权后，App 读取相册中最新的截图（或你手动选择的图片），用于识别文字。文字识别（iOS Vision / Android ML Kit）和翻译（Apple 翻译框架 / ML Kit 翻译）均在设备本机运行。截图和照片不会上传到我们的服务器或任何第三方。首次翻译时，系统可能从 Apple 或 Google 下载离线语言包，这一过程由操作系统完成，不包含你的截图内容。

保存在本机的数据

历史记录（截图副本、识别文字、译文）、生词本和收藏的句子保存在 App 的本机存储中。删除 App 即全部清除；也可以在 App 内长按删除单条记录。

账号

核心功能无需登录即可使用。你可以选择用 Apple 或 Google 登录，以便在多台设备间同步。登录后我们会收集：你的邮箱地址和账号 ID（由 Apple / Google 提供），以及你的生词本、收藏的句子、历史记录中的识别文字与译文。这些数据仅用于同步功能，与你的账号关联，经加密传输，存储在我们使用的云服务 Supabase 上。截图图片本身不会上传。不登录则上述数据全部只保存在本机。

我们不做的事

  • 不投放广告，不使用广告追踪或跨 App 追踪；
  • 不出售或分享你的个人数据；
  • 不上传截图、照片；不将数据用于追踪。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集个人信息。

删除数据

未登录时，所有数据只在你的手机上，删除 App 即全部清除；也可在 App 内长按删除单条记录。已登录的用户如需删除账号及云端数据，请发邮件至 josh.zeng.ca@gmail.com，我们会在 30 天内删除。

更新与联系

政策更新会在本页公布并更新生效日期。问题请联系 josh.zeng.ca@gmail.com。

Overview

截图译 (Screenshot Translator) is developed by Joshua Zeng (JoshLabs, Canada). Your screenshots are processed on your device and never uploaded.

Photos and screenshots

With your permission, the app reads your most recent screenshot (or an image you pick) to recognize text. Text recognition (iOS Vision / Android ML Kit) and translation (Apple Translation / ML Kit Translation) run on your device. Screenshots and photos are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. The operating system may download offline language packs from Apple or Google; this does not include your screenshot content.

Data stored on your device

History (screenshot copies, recognized text, translations), your wordbook, and saved sentences are stored locally. Deleting the app removes them; you can also delete individual items in the app.

Accounts

All core features work without an account. You may optionally sign in with Apple or Google to sync across devices. When signed in, we collect your email address and account ID (provided by Apple / Google), plus your wordbook, saved sentences, and the recognized text and translations in your history. This data is used only for sync, is linked to your account, is encrypted in transit, and is stored with our cloud provider Supabase. Screenshot images are never uploaded. If you do not sign in, all of this stays on your device only.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your personal data.
  • We don't upload your screenshots or photos, and we don't use your data for tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Delete your data

If you are not signed in, all data stays on your phone and deleting the app removes it; you can also delete individual items in the app. If you are signed in and want your account and cloud data deleted, email josh.zeng.ca@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.
