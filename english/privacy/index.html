概述

English150 Publisher 是 JoshLabs（Joshua Zeng）出品的个人内容发布工具，用于将英语学习短视频自动发布到 TikTok 账号。本工具仅供账号所有者本人使用，不面向公众开放，也不收集任何第三方用户的个人信息。

我们访问的数据

  • TikTok 账号授权：本工具通过 TikTok 官方 OAuth 获取账号授权，用于代表账号所有者发布视频。OAuth 令牌（Access Token）仅存储在本地设备上，不上传至任何第三方服务器。
  • 视频与 caption 内容：视频文件和发布文案通过 TikTok Content Posting API 上传至 TikTok，受 TikTok 自身的服务条款与隐私政策约束。

我们不收集的内容

  • 任何第三方用户的个人信息。
  • 广告标识符或跨应用追踪数据。
  • 账号密码或支付信息。

数据存储

所有数据（OAuth 令牌、发布状态记录）均存储在运行本工具的本地设备上，不上传至 JoshLabs 的服务器。

第三方服务

  • TikTok — 视频发布平台，受 TikTok 隐私政策约束。

Overview

English150 Publisher is a personal content publishing tool by JoshLabs (Joshua Zeng) for automatically uploading English learning short videos to a TikTok account. This tool is operated solely by the account owner and is not open to the public. It does not collect personal information from any third-party users.

Data we access

  • TikTok account authorization — The tool uses TikTok's official OAuth flow to obtain authorization to post videos on behalf of the account owner. OAuth access tokens are stored only on the local device and are not sent to any third-party server.
  • Video and caption content — Video files and captions are uploaded to TikTok via the TikTok Content Posting API and are subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

  • Personal information of any third-party user.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data.
  • Account passwords or payment information.

Data storage

All data (OAuth tokens, publishing status records) is stored on the local device running this tool. Nothing is uploaded to JoshLabs servers.

Third-party services

  • TikTok — The video publishing platform. Subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy.
