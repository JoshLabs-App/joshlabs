Transfer · 传输 · v1.0.3

PhotoPorter

LAN file transfer between Android phones, iPhone, and Mac — photos, videos, and hand-picked files over Wi‑Fi or hotspot. No cloud, no account.

安卓手机、iPhone、Mac 之间的局域网传输工具：照片、视频、手选文件，经 Wi‑Fi 或热点互传。没有账号、没有公网中继。

Android APK · 安卓安装包 iPhone · App Store Mac · App Store Privacy · 隐私 Support · 支持

Android — send & receive: pair with other Android phones, iPhones, or a Mac inbox (APK · Play). iPhone — send to an Android or Mac inbox (PhotoPorter X). Mac — receive inbox from Android & iPhone senders (PhotoPorter Mac).
安卓 — 可发可收，与安卓、iPhone、Mac 收箱互传。 iPhone — 发送到安卓或 Mac 收箱机。 Mac — 收箱机，接收安卓与 iPhone 发送端。

Platforms · 各端

One protocol · 同一套协议

Android · 安卓

Send and receive. Copy from other Android phones or iPhones onto this phone, or send to another Android phone or Mac inbox. 可发可收：从其他安卓或 iPhone 拷到本机，或发到另一台安卓或 Mac 收箱。

iPhone

Send photos, videos, and hand-picked files to an Android phone or Mac inbox on the same Wi‑Fi. Scan the receiver QR once. 发送到同一 Wi‑Fi 上的安卓手机或 Mac 收箱机；扫码配对一次即可。

Mac

Receive inbox for your desk. Android and iPhone senders scan the Mac QR code; files land in Pictures, Movies, or Downloads/PhoneInbox. 桌面收箱机：安卓与 iPhone 发送端扫 Mac 二维码，文件落入对应文件夹。

What transfers · 传什么

Camera rolls (photos & videos together), plus one-shot sends of files or a whole folder. Checksum before ACK — copy, not cloud sync. 相册原样（照片视频一起），也可手选文件或目录发一次；校验通过才确认，是拷贝不是云同步。

How to use · 怎么用

Wi‑Fi or hotspot

1. Receive · 接收

Open PhotoPorter on the inbox (Android phone, Mac, or another Android). 收箱机打开 App（安卓手机或 Mac），选「接收」。

2. Pair · 配对

On camera phones choose Send and scan the QR code. 相机手机选「发送」，扫二维码配对。

3. Auto copy · 自动拷

While the app is in the foreground, new photos and videos (or selected albums) copy over. App 在前台时，新照片新视频（或你选中的相册）自动拷过去。

4. Send once · 发一次

Or hand-pick files, a folder, or a few album items for a one-shot transfer. 也可手选文件、整个目录或相册里几项，发完即止。

Features · 功能

Android · iPhone · Mac

Send or receive · 发送 / 接收

Phones send; Android phone or Mac receives. One protocol, same QR pairing. 手机发送；安卓手机或 Mac 接收。同一协议、同一套二维码配对。

Albums as-is · 相册原样

Whatever is in the album goes — photos and videos together. No type switch to forget.

LAN only · 仅局域网

Traffic stays on Wi‑Fi or the sender’s hotspot. There is no public relay.

Pair once · 配对一次

Scan the receiver QR (or type the code). Token sticks when the IP changes.

Checksum ACK · 校验确认

Marked sent only after the receiver writes the file and verifies SHA‑256.

Send once · 手选发送

Hand-pick files, a folder, or a few album items for a one-shot transfer.

English & 中文

Follows the system language on Android, iPhone, and Mac.

Copy, not sync · 拷贝非同步

A one-way copy into the inbox folders — not two-way album sync.
