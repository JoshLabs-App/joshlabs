Philosophy

01

No cloud in between.

Transfers stay on your Wi‑Fi or the sender's hotspot. There is no public relay, no server log, no third-party storage. What leaves your phone lands directly on the receiver.

传输在局域网里发生，没有公网中继，没有服务器日志，没有第三方存储。

02

Scan once, transfer always.

Pair two devices by scanning a QR code once. The token persists even when the IP changes — reconnect automatically, no re-scanning. Albums move with one tap.

扫码配对一次，Token 在 IP 变动后仍然有效，自动重连，相册一键发送。

03

Verified before it's done.

Every file is marked sent only after the receiver writes it and confirms the SHA‑256 checksum. Copy, not sync. What arrives is exactly what left.

每个文件经 SHA-256 校验通过后才标记完成。拷贝，不是同步；到达即原样。

Camera rolls (photos & videos together), plus one-shot sends of files or a whole folder. Checksum before ACK.
